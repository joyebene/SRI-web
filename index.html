About Scale Up Africa

Starting in Abuja. Growing across Africa.

One initiative. A continent of possibilities.

About the Event

Helping young Africans turn potential into progress.

Scale Up Africa is a conference built for young Africans who are ready to grow. Too many talented people and promising businesses stay small, not for lack of ideas, but for lack of the right information and the right people around them.

Scale Up Africa closes that gap. Through practical talks, workshops and real conversations, we equip you with the knowledge, skills and connections you need to take what you do to the next level.

This edition holds in Abuja, Nigeria, with more states and African countries to follow. Abuja is the starting point of a growing vision to reach more people, create meaningful connections and unlock opportunities across the continent.
